Foundations

Why Leaving Cash Idle Costs You Money

Money sitting in a basic locker or low-interest account loses 5-7% of its value every year due to inflation. Here is the difference between saving and smart investing.

🛡️

1. Capital Protection

Your hard-earned savings need a secure home protected by bank insurance (DICGC up to ₹5 Lakh) or sovereign Government of India backing.

📈

2. Beating Inflation

When milk, fuel, and groceries get 6% more expensive each year, your money must grow at 7% to 14% to ensure your future purchasing power expands.

⏳

3. The Time Horizon

Matching your money to the right duration prevents premature withdrawals and maximizes compound interest over months, years, and decades.

Smart Habits

3 Golden Rules for Beginners

Follow these timeless financial principles before making your first investment.

1

Build an Emergency Fund First

Before investing in long-term options like Equity or PPF, save 3 to 6 months of living expenses in a liquid Savings Account or Liquid Mutual Fund so you never have to break investments during emergencies.

2

Understand the Lock-in Period

Never lock money in PPF (15 years) or multi-year Fixed Deposits if you will need cash in the next few months. Match each financial goal with the correct investment timeline.

3

Start Small, Stay Consistent

You don't need lakhs to begin. A monthly recurring deposit (RD) or mutual fund SIP of even ₹500/month started today beats waiting years to accumulate a large lump sum.
